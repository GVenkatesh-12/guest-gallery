On-Device Processing

Information We Collect§1

We collect no personal information. Guest Gallery is a fully offline application:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No photos, images, or media are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored by us.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No personal data — such as name, email, location, or device identifiers — is collected.

How the App Works§2

Guest Gallery lets you temporarily share selected photos with a guest. The photos you select are read directly from your device's storage and displayed locally in a secure, sandboxed viewer. They remain on your device and are never transferred outside of it.

Permissions§3

The app requests only the permissions required to function — nothing more:

Photo / media accessUsed solely to display the photos you choose to share — and nothing else.
Fingerprint / device PINUsed only to verify the device owner when exiting the guest session.

Internet and Network§4

Guest Gallery is engineered without the Internet permission in its application manifest. This means the app is physically incapable of connecting to a network, sending data, or leaking information. Your photos never leave your device.

Data Storage and Security§5

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing stored on any server. Photos shown during a session remain in your own device storage, fully under your control. The guest viewer is ephemeral: no library, timeline, or persistent records are created.

Third-Party Services§6

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, cloud providers, or data processors.

Children's Privacy§7

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your Rights§8

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. All data remains on your device, which you can manage at any time.

Changes to This Policy§9

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
