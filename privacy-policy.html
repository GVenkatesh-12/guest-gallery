Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Guest Gallery ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Guest Gallery is a fully offline application:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No photos, images, or media are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored by us.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No personal data such as name, email, location, or device identifiers is collected.

2. How the App Works

Guest Gallery lets you temporarily share selected photos with a guest. The photos you select are only read directly from your device's storage and displayed locally. They remain on your device and are never transferred outside of it.

3. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions required to function:

  • Photo/media access — used solely to display the photos you choose to share, and nothing else.
  • Fingerprint / device PIN — used only to verify the device owner when exiting the guest session.

4. Internet and Network

Guest Gallery is engineered without the Internet permission. It is physically incapable of connecting to a network, sending data, or leaking information. Your photos never leave your device.

5. Data Storage and Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing stored on any server. Any photos shown remain in your own device storage under your control.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, cloud providers, or data processors.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Your Rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. All data remains on your device, which you can manage at any time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
